Long-term Vision Project

Kenadsa Gardens Oasis

While our day-to-day work focuses on transactional logistics and dashboard engineering, we believe AI systems can act as a catalyst for global sustainability. Kenadsa Gardens Oasis is our long-term research vision project.

Located on the edge of the Sahara, Kenadsa represents a historical crossroads of irrigation and resilience. Our project models digital-twin automation for arid-zone agriculture. By simulating microclimates, analyzing localized water evaporation, and deploying automated smart-drip networks, we study how technology can sustainably restore drylands.
